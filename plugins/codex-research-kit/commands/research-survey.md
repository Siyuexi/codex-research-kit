# /research-survey

Use the `research-survey` skill.

Interpret `$ARGUMENTS` as the survey topic, sub-topic, or project operation. This command is scoped to research-workflow projects and should produce structured survey artifacts, candidate lists, and log entries according to the skill.

Required behavior:

1. Confirm the active research-workflow project and target sub-topic.
2. Use first-principles decomposition before collecting sources.
3. Prefer primary sources and source-grounded claims.
4. Write outputs into the project's research artifacts and log the sweep.
5. Surface uncertainty and source gaps instead of padding the survey.
